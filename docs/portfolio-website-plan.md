# Portfolio website plan (draft)

Anastasia M. · UI/UX designer · Human–AI interaction

## 1.Positioning

I design AI-assisted interfaces.Turning AI features into screens built around the person using them.

## 2.Pages

- ### Home (built)

  One scrolling page: hero, the four projects, and the footer with contact. Each project appears as a summary with its question, its decision, and its tags.
- ### Project pages (open)

  Not in the current build. Each project is planned to have its own page, with the evidence, the decisions and rejected alternatives, and the screens. Decide whether the home summaries link to them.
- ### Contact

  Contact is an email link in the footer, reached from the “Contact” link at the top.

## 3.Home page blocks

1. ### 1. Top bar

   Name on the left, “Projects” and “Contact” on the right. The name is a placeholder: \[Name\] \[Surname\].
2. ### 2. Hero

   The headline “I design AI-assisted interfaces” in large type, with two short lines: the positioning sentence and a line saying each of the four projects is shown from the first question to the final screens. No button. A bottom bar carries the role, “UI/UX designer, Human–AI interaction”, a “Scroll down” cue, and the creative element: four dots in the project colors that light up in turn while a ring travels across them.
3. ### 3. Projects

   A “Projects” heading with a four-dot marker, then a numbered side rail (01 to 04) that highlights the project in view. Each project is a full-width block in its own color, with a meta line (type · status · year), the title and subtitle, a Question and a Decision, three tags, and a framed screenshot. On scroll, the Decision drops into place.
4. ### 4. Footer

   A large email link, the name, and a “Back to top” button with the four dots. The email is a placeholder: \[email@address\].

## 4.The four projects as built

1. ### 01 · Miri

   AI concierge · Mobile app · Concept · 2026

   Question: How should the concierge get to know a guest who doesn’t want to be asked questions? Decision: preferences come from behavior, not questionnaires, and it offers one suggestion at a time.

   Tags: Human–AI interaction, Korean service culture, Design system. Screenshot: yes.
2. ### 02 · RentFlow

   Property management platform · Web app · Concept · 2026

   Question: How should AI speak to an owner, a tenant and a manager about the same property? Decision: each role gets its own dashboard, where AI suggests next to the numbers and the person decides.

   Tags: Human–AI interaction, Dashboards, Information architecture. Screenshot: yes.
3. ### 03 · Studio

   AI concept boards · Web app · In progress · 2026

   Question: How can a designer use AI to assemble a concept board and still feel that every choice is theirs? Decision: the AI only searches and combines real catalog items; the designer swaps any piece, the budget rebalances, and the client approves.

   Tags: Human–AI interaction, Design tools, Real product data. Screenshot: not yet, the card shows “Screens in progress”.
4. ### 04 · SeMA

   Museum website redesign · Web redesign · Concept · 2026

   Question: What happens when the findings of a UX audit are applied to an existing museum website? Decision: the audit set the direction, and the redesign followed it through the navigation, the exhibitions and the collection page.

   Tags: UX audit, Navigation redesign, Visual hierarchy. Screenshot: yes.

The project order is Miri, RentFlow, Studio, SeMA.
